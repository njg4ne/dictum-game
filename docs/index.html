How to play

Guess the Latin word in six tries. Type with your keyboard or the keys on the screen, and press Enter to guess.

  • Green: the right letter in the right place.
  • Yellow: the letter is in the word, but somewhere else.
  • Grey: the letter is not in the word.

The answer is a form of a word from the current list (a noun in a particular case, a verb in a particular tense, and so on). Any five-letter Latin form counts as a guess, even one that is not on the list. A guess that isn't Latin is refused and costs no turn.

Daily word is the same word for everyone each day. Streaks keeps giving new words until you miss one. Under the keyboard, the hint buttons show the word's grammar, its meaning, or the answer itself.
